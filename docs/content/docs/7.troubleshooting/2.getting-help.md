## Ask the community
| Where | Use it for |
|-------|------------|
| [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/serversideup/docker-php/discussions){target="_blank"} | Questions, setup help, and feature ideas. Answers stay searchable for the next person. |
| [Discord](https://serversideup.net/discord){target="_blank"} | Quick questions and real-time chat |
| [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/serversideup/docker-php/issues/new){target="_blank"} | Reproducible bugs, where the image doesn't behave as documented |

Search Discussions before you post. Please don't open an issue for a general question. To report a security vulnerability, follow the [security policy](https://github.com/serversideup/docker-php/security/policy){target="_blank"} instead of opening a public issue.

When you ask, include:

- The image tag, such as `serversideup/php:8.5-fpm-nginx`
- Your `compose.yml` or `Dockerfile`
- The full output of `docker compose logs`
- What you expected, what happened, and what you've tried

[How to ask for help](https://serversideup.net/ask-for-help/){target="_blank"} has more on writing a question that gets answered.

## Paid support
The maintainers offer paid help:

- [A one-hour session](https://schedule.serversideup.net/team/serversideup/quick-chat-with-jay){target="_blank"} with screen sharing, for a specific question or problem.
- [Ongoing development and support](https://serversideup.net/hire-us/){target="_blank"} for teams that need continuing help with Docker, deployment, or their application.
